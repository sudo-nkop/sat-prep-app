# Answer Key — Math Practice 2: Advanced Math

**1. B** — Factor: `(x − 2)(x − 3) = 0` → `x = 2, 3`.

**2. B** — `x² − 4x + 3x − 12 = x² − x − 12`.

**3. A** — `f(2) = 4 − 8 + 3 = −1`.

**4. C** — Multiply coefficients (6) and add exponents (3+4=7): `6x⁷`.

**5. B** — `2^(x+1) = 32 = 2⁵` → `x + 1 = 5` → `x = 4`.

**6. A** — Vertex form `(x − 3)² − 4`: minimum is at the vertex, which is **−4**.

**7. B** — `x² − 9 = (x − 3)(x + 3)`. Cancel `(x − 3)` → `x + 3`.

**8. B** — Roots at −2 and 5 → factors `(x + 2)(x − 5)`.

**9. 8** — `x² − 6x − 16 = 0` → `(x − 8)(x + 2) = 0` → positive root is 8.

**10. C** — Square both sides: `2x + 1 = 25` → `x = 12`.

**11. D** — `x − 4 = ±7` → `x = 11` or `x = −3`. Sum = 8.

**12. B** — Negative exponent rule: `x⁻² = 1/x²`.

**13. A** — Roots at 1 and 5 means `y = a(x − 1)(x − 5)`. Plug (0, 5): `5 = a(−1)(−5) = 5a` → `a = 1`. So `y = (x − 1)(x − 5)`.

**14. 2** — `3x² − 2x − 8 = 0`. Quadratic formula: `x = (2 ± √(4 + 96))/6 = (2 ± 10)/6`. Positive: `x = 2`.

**15. B** — One x-intercept means discriminant = 0: `b² − 36 = 0` → `b = ±6`.
