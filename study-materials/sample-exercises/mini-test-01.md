# Sample Mini-Test 1 (Mixed Section)

A mini timed test mixing R&W and Math. **Time yourself**: 25 minutes total.

Answers in `answer-keys/08-mini-test-01-answers.md`. Or just use the app — it's faster.

## Reading & Writing (10 questions, ~12 min)

**1.** The chef ___ the dish before serving it to the guests.

A) taste   B) tastes   C) tasting   D) have tasted

---

**2.** ___ the rain, the picnic was canceled.

A) Despite   B) Because of   C) However   D) Although

---

**3.** "Mitochondria are often called the powerhouse of the cell because they produce ATP, the molecule that fuels nearly every cellular process."

The author's primary purpose is to:

A) Compare mitochondria to other organelles
B) Explain the role of mitochondria in producing energy
C) Argue that mitochondria are the most important organelle
D) Describe the structure of ATP

---

**4.** Choose the most concise option.

A) On account of the fact that
B) Due to the fact that
C) Because of the reason that
D) Because

---

**5.** The Pulitzer Prize-winning author ___ several novels before her debut was widely recognized.

A) writes   B) had written   C) was writing   D) is writing

---

**6.** ___ the team's strong defense, they lost the game.

A) Although   B) Despite   C) Whereas   D) Even

---

**7.** "Studies suggest that students who get adequate sleep perform better academically. ___, sleep deprivation has been linked to lower test scores."

A) Furthermore   B) However   C) Specifically   D) On the other hand

---

**8.** Choose the correctly punctuated sentence.

A) The novel which I read last summer was excellent.
B) The novel, which I read last summer was excellent.
C) The novel, which I read last summer, was excellent.
D) The novel; which I read last summer, was excellent.

---

**9.** "The researchers concluded the experiment ___ inconclusive results."

A) , however
B) yielded
C) ;
D) :

---

**10.** Notes:

- Octopuses have nine brains (one central, eight in the arms).
- They can change skin color and texture in seconds.
- They are considered among the most intelligent invertebrates.

Goal: emphasize the **biological uniqueness** of octopuses. Best choice?

A) Octopuses are intelligent invertebrates.
B) Octopuses can change color in seconds.
C) Octopuses have nine brains and can rapidly change their skin color and texture, making them among the most biologically unique invertebrates.
D) Octopuses are studied by biologists.
