# Test Day Strategy Cheatsheet

## Night before
- Pack: ID, admission ticket, charged laptop/tablet (Bluebook), charger, snacks, water, watch.
- Charge **and** bring the charger.
- Sleep 7+ hours. Don't cram new material.

## Morning of
- Eat protein + complex carbs (eggs, oats). Avoid sugar crashes.
- Caffeine only if you normally drink it.
- Arrive 30 min early.

## During the test

### Pacing rules
- **R&W**: 27 questions in 32 min ≈ 70 sec/question.
- **Math**: 22 questions in 35 min ≈ 95 sec/question.
- Easy questions first, hard last in each module — but **all questions are worth the same**. Don't sink 5 minutes on one tough question while leaving easy ones unanswered.

### Skip-and-return
1. First pass: answer every question you can do in <90 sec.
2. **Mark** the rest with the flag tool.
3. Second pass: hit flagged questions.
4. Last 60 sec: **fill in every blank** — there's no penalty for guessing.

### Math-specific
- **Use Desmos**. If you can graph it, do. Faster than algebra for many questions.
- For "which value of k...": use Desmos sliders.
- For word problems: define variables first, write the equation, *then* solve.
- For "could be," plug in answer choices.
- Always re-read the question before bubbling — they ask for `x+2`, not `x`.

### Reading & Writing-specific
- For grammar: identify the **issue** (punctuation? agreement?) before looking at choices.
- For reading: predict in your own words first.
- For transitions: classify the relationship (add/contrast/cause), then match.

### Mental reset
- If you panic, close your eyes for 5 seconds. Breathe. Move on. The test is adaptive — one wrong answer doesn't kill your score, but ten minutes lost spiraling does.

## Module 2 adaptive note
- Module 2 difficulty depends on Module 1.
- Hard Module 2 = good sign (you're in the higher-scoring track).
- Don't let "this seems hard" psyche you out.

## Score targets

| Score | Roughly required correct |
|---|---|
| 1200 | ~70% across both sections |
| 1400 | ~85% |
| 1500 | ~92% |
| 1550+ | near-perfect, ≤1–2 misses per section |
