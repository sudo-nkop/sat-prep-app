# Math Practice 3 — Problem Solving & Data Analysis

15 questions: ratios, percents, statistics, units, probability. Answers in `answer-keys/03-problem-solving-answers.md`.

---

**1.** A shirt is on sale for 25% off its original price of $48. What is the sale price?

A) $12   B) $23   C) $36   D) $40

---

**2.** A recipe uses 3 cups of flour for every 2 cups of sugar. If you use 9 cups of flour, how many cups of sugar do you need?

A) 4   B) 5   C) 6   D) 8

---

**3.** The mean of five numbers is 14. Four of the numbers are 10, 12, 15, and 18. What is the fifth number?

A) 13   B) 14   C) 15   D) 16

---

**4.** A car travels 240 miles in 4 hours. At the same rate, how many miles will it travel in 7 hours?

A) 280   B) 360   C) 420   D) 480

---

**5.** Last year, a town's population grew from 12,000 to 13,200. By what percent did the population grow?

A) 1%   B) 8%   C) 10%   D) 12%

---

**6.** A bag contains 4 red marbles, 5 blue marbles, and 6 green marbles. What is the probability of drawing a blue marble at random?

A) 1/3   B) 5/15   C) 4/15   D) 6/15

(Hint: A and B are the same — eliminate both, then it's between C and D.)

*Corrected:* A) 1/4   B) 1/3   C) 4/15   D) 6/15
