**2.** A student is writing about renewable energy and has gathered the following notes:

- Solar power use in the U.S. grew 25% from 2020 to 2023.
- Wind power use grew 18% in the same period.
- Both sources still account for less than 15% of total U.S. energy generation.

The student wants to **emphasize the limited role of solar and wind despite recent growth**. Which choice best uses the notes?

A) Solar and wind power use grew significantly between 2020 and 2023.
B) Although solar and wind power use grew rapidly between 2020 and 2023, the two sources still account for less than 15% of U.S. energy generation.
C) Solar power grew 25% and wind power grew 18% between 2020 and 2023.
D) Wind and solar are the fastest-growing energy sources in the U.S.

**4.** A student is researching coral reef conservation and has these notes:

- Coral reefs cover less than 1% of the ocean floor.
- They support roughly 25% of all marine species.
- Rising ocean temperatures have caused widespread bleaching.
- Many reefs have lost more than half of their coral cover since 1980.

Which choice best **emphasizes the disproportionate ecological importance of reefs**?

A) Coral reefs cover less than 1% of the ocean floor but support roughly 25% of all marine species.
B) Rising ocean temperatures have caused widespread bleaching of coral reefs.
C) Many coral reefs have lost more than half of their coral cover since 1980.
D) Coral reefs are home to many species of fish.
