## WRITING - Possessives and Apostrophes (Section: writing, Topic: possessives)

Q166: "Photographer Ansel Adams's landscape portraits are iconic pieces of American art. However, many of the _______ were intended not as art but as marketing."
A: photographers early photo's
B: photographers early photos
C: photographer's early photos
D: photographer's early photo's
Answer: C | Difficulty: 2 | Explain: "Photographer's" (singular possessive) correctly shows that the photos belong to one photographer. "Photos" is a simple plural with no apostrophe. C is the only option with both correct forms.

Q167: "Inventor John Friedman created a flexible straw by inserting a screw into a paper straw and binding the straw tightly around the _______ When the floss and screw were removed, the corrugations allowed the straw to bend."
A: screw's threads.
B: screws' threads.
C: screw's threads.
D: screws threads'.
Answer: A | Difficulty: 2 | Explain: One screw is being described, requiring the singular possessive "screw's." "Screws'" would indicate multiple screws, and "screws threads'" is grammatically incorrect.

Q168: "Beatrix Potter dedicated herself to mycology, the study of _______ more than 350 paintings of the fungal species she observed in nature."

B: fungi. Producing
C: fungi producing
D: fungi, producing
Answer: D | Difficulty: 3 | Explain: "Producing" is a participial phrase that continues the sentence's description of what Potter did. A comma before "producing" correctly introduces this participial phrase that modifies the subject.

Q169: "The striated _____ walls are the result of erosion and frost weathering having exposed layers of multicolored sedimentary rock."

B: patterns of a canyons'
C: pattern's of a canyon's
D: patterns of a canyon's
Answer: D | Difficulty: 3 | Explain: "Patterns" is a simple plural (no possession). "Canyon's" is a singular possessive, correctly indicating the walls belong to one canyon. D correctly uses plural "patterns" and singular possessive "canyon's."
